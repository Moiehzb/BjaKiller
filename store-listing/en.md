## Description complète

Enter the Secret Academy of Counters.

HI-LO Academy I is a blackjack card counting trainer. Card after card, you learn to keep the Hi-Lo count — the reference technique — until it becomes a reflex.

THE HI-LO SYSTEM
Every card is worth one point: +1 for 2 through 6, 0 for 7 through 9, −1 for tens, face cards and Aces. Keep the running count without ever losing it, at increasingly demanding speeds.
The app teaches you the method, then pushes you toward perfection.

THE PATHS OF THE ACADEMY
• Study Hall — free training. Set the number of decks, penetration and speed. Start gently with The Decipherment, then speed up.
• The Guild Gates — ranked mode. Climb six ranks, from Cuivre to Adamantium, hand after hand, counter hidden.
• The Trial — five chained circles, from a single-deck shoe to an eight-deck one. A single mistake and everything starts over.
• Daily Rite — a single challenge, the same for everyone, renewed every day. Keep your streak.

PROGRESSION
Six ranks, eighteen tiers, a speed curve that takes you from a beginner's comfort to an expert's lightning pace. Unlock Deeds — demanding feats, counter sealed — and earn the Seal of the Certified Counter.

ARTIFACTS
Dress your cards in Artifact sets, from the free Classic to the rarest. Some are earned by playing; the most precious are acquired from the Merchant. One last one lies hidden.

BUILT TO LAST
• Fully offline — your progress stays on your device.
• Fourteen languages.
• Original music, sound effects and haptic feedback, all can be disabled.
• No ads, no account to create. No betting, no real money — only your memory and your discipline.

Card counting is a skill: focus, memory and a lot of practice. HI-LO Academy I is your training room.

The threshold is open. Time to count.
